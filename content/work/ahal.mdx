## The problem

Understanding an unfamiliar codebase can take hours or days. Reviewers, new teammates and judges usually need the shape of a project before its details.

## The approach

AHAL AI V2 analyses repositories, documents and source code, then produces executive summaries, problem statements, architecture understanding and a conversation about the project. Gemma 4 is the reasoning layer.
