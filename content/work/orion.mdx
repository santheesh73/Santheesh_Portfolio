## The problem

Most AI assistants send every conversation and every document to someone else's server. For private notes and files, that is the wrong default.

## The approach

Orion runs the model in the browser. WebLLM uses WebGPU for local inference, and everything the assistant knows about you lives in IndexedDB on your own device.

- **Local inference** through WebLLM and WebGPU.
- **Local memory**: chats, documents and settings persist in IndexedDB via Dexie.
- **Document intelligence**: upload, parse, chunk, search and chat with local files.
- **Worker isolation**: generation and parsing run in Web Workers, off the main thread.
- **Offline-first PWA** with zero telemetry.
