## The problem

Audit preparation is slow and reactive. GST mismatches, missing invoices and late filings tend to surface during the audit itself, when they are most expensive to fix.

## The approach

PRYSM is a continuous audit intelligence layer for finance teams, CFOs, CA firms and SMEs. Models read documents and explain findings; deterministic rules decide what is a finding.

- **Extract.** PyMuPDF and Tesseract pull text from native and scanned PDFs.
- **Read.** An LLM extracts entities: GSTIN, invoice numbers, dates, amounts, HSN codes.
- **Decide.** 18 deterministic compliance rules flag gaps and mismatches.
- **Rank.** A risk dashboard sorts findings into Critical, High, Medium and Low.
- **Explain.** An AI copilot explains each risk in plain English, and the report exports as an audit-ready PDF.

ChromaDB adds semantic search across the uploaded files.
