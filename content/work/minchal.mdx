## The problem

Most households get an electricity bill with one number on it and no idea which appliance is driving it. Appliance-level monitoring needs smart plugs, meters or current clamps, which most families will not buy.

MINCHAL starts with something every household already has: the bill.

## The approach

> Gemini reads the bill. The deterministic engine calculates. Gemini explains.

A language model should not calculate money. MINCHAL uses Gemini only where perception or language is needed, and keeps every number inside a deterministic Python engine.

- **Read.** Gemini Vision extracts consumption units, the amount, the billing period and the tariff from a photographed bill.
- **Calculate.** The engine estimates each appliance's kWh from rated wattage, hours, duty cycle, star rating, age and observed symptoms.
- **Normalise.** Estimates are scaled so their sum matches the units on the real bill.
- **Plan.** Fixes and habits are ranked by return on investment and chosen within the household's budget.
- **Explain.** Gemini writes the audit summary in English or Tamil.
